Auto-Upgrader Button Should Be Visible Earlier
==============================================

STATUS: COMPLETE
================
Fixed in buttons.js line 342. Removed `G.magic >= 10` from showFn.
The button now shows once any button has 3+ upgrades.
The displayCost:10 already makes the button disabled until you have 10 magic.

---

Problem:
The auto-upgrader button (costs 10 magic) is hidden until you have 10 magic.
It should be visible once you've upgraded any button 3 times, even if you
don't have 10 magic yet.

Current behavior:
- Button hidden until G.magic >= 10 AND 3+ upgrades on some button

Desired behavior:
- Button visible once any button has 3+ upgrades
- Button disabled/greyed out if G.magic < 10
- Button enabled once G.magic >= 10

This lets players know the feature exists and what they're working toward.

File to fix:
- buttons.js: auto_upgrader showFn (around line 342)

Current code:
  showFn: function(){return G.magicOn && G.magic >= 10 && Object.keys(G.upgradeLevels).some(function(k){return G.upgradeLevels[k]>=3})}

Should change to just check magicOn and upgrades, not magic count.
Then costFn or the click handler needs to enforce the 10 magic requirement.
