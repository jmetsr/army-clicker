Dark Ritual Changes:

1. Practice Mode Enemy
   - Practice mode now gives you an enemy

2. Un-vanquish Mechanic
   - If enemy was vanquished, dark ritual un-vanquishes it
   - Once first dark ritual is bought, enemy stays un-vanquished FOREVER
   - Even if you vanquish it again, it can still spawn more troops

3. Dragon Spawning Formula
   For every day d after your nth dark ritual purchase, enemy gets d^n dragons

   Example: Dark rituals bought on days 100, 200, 300, 400
   On day 500, enemy gets:
   - (500-100)^1 = 400^1 from 1st ritual
   - (500-200)^2 = 300^2 from 2nd ritual
   - (500-300)^3 = 200^3 from 3rd ritual
   - (500-400)^4 = 100^4 from 4th ritual
   Total: 400 + 90,000 + 8,000,000 + 100,000,000 dragons

4. Dragon Mechanics
   - Dragons spawn regardless of enemy's other troop types
   - Even if enemy troops degraded to thugs or upgraded, dragons stay dragons
   - Works for enemy levels 1, 2, 3 - they just accumulate and use in battles

5. AI Enemy Income
   - These dragons generate income for AI enemy
   - If AI starves or loses battle, loses proportionally from ALL troop types
   - e.g., 50% loss = 50% of dragons AND 50% of other troops

6. UI Warning
   - Dark ritual button should warn "this helps your enemies"
