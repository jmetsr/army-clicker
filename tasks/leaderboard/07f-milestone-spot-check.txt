Spot-Check Milestone Claims Against Log
========================================

GOAL: Verify that claimed milestones (day reached 1M, etc.) actually
happened in the log at that day.

TASKS:

1. For each claimed milestone, find that day in log
   - dayMillionCoins: 50 → check log around day 50
   - dayVanquish: 200 → check log around day 200

2. Verify coin milestones
   - Find coin balance at claimed day
   - Verify it crosses the threshold
   - Check day before was below threshold (first time)

3. Verify event milestones
   - dayVanquish: log shows enemy defeated event
   - daySurrender: log shows surrender event

4. Allow small tolerance
   - If they claim day 50 but log shows day 51, that's OK
   - Maybe ±1 day tolerance for rounding/timing

5. Output
   - List of milestones that don't match log
   - What the log actually shows

DEPENDENCIES:
- Task 07a (parsed log)
- Task 07c-ordinal-number (to compare thresholds)

ESTIMATE: 1-2 hours

NOTES:
- Much simpler than full economic replay
- Just checks specific moments, not entire history
- Good bang for buck - catches milestone inflation
- Could do this WITHOUT full economic validation
