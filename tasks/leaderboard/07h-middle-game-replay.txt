Middle Game Replay Validation (Magic Stage)
============================================

GOAL: Extend the replay engine to handle the magic stage of the game.
This covers magic unlocking through to auto-upgraders (but not including
double-digit magic cost items).

SCOPE:

- Rituals (single-digit magic costs)
- Dragon mechanics (enemy dragons, dragon battles)
- Extended army tiers unlocked by magic
- NOT: Auto-upgraders, Forbidden Ritual tier (these cost 10+ magic)
