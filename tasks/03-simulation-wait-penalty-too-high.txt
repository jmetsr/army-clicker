Fix Simulation AI Wait Penalty
==============================

Location: simulation/ folder (DO NOT modify game AI until approved)

Problem:
It's too easy for human to beat AI by delaying strong buildings until later.
The AI is winning in the short term but human wins long-term.

Suspected Cause:
The wait penalty is too high. AI penalizes future buildings at ~0.01/day, meaning
anything it can't build for 100+ days gets ignored. This makes AI too short-sighted.

Evidence:
- AI always wins short term
- Human pulls ahead if they wait long enough
- This suggests AI is undervaluing long-term investments

Potential Fixes:
1. Reduce the wait penalty rate (e.g., 0.001/day instead of 0.01/day)
2. Use exponential decay instead of linear penalty
3. Add special handling for high-value long-term buildings
4. Implement look-ahead to see how building A enables building B

Testing:
- Run simulations comparing current vs modified penalty
- Verify AI now considers buildings 100+ days out
- Ensure human can't trivially win by just waiting

IMPORTANT: Only modify simulation/ folder code, not game AI, until human approves.
